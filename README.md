 Consumer Behaviour Analysis Project

This project presents a comprehensive consumer behaviour analysis using transactional data from 3,900 retail purchases across various product categories. The primary goal is to uncover spending patterns, customer segments, product preferences, and subscription behaviours to guide strategic business decisions.

Key Features & Workflow

Data Preparation & Cleaning:** Performed using Python (Pandas & NumPy) to standardise columns to snake_case, create age groups, and handle missing review ratings.

Relational Database Management:** Cleaned transactional data loaded into PostgreSQL to perform structured SQL queries and extract deep business insights.

Executive Dashboard Summary:** Tracks core performance metrics including total revenue (₹22.4M), average purchase amount (₹5,737), customer count (3.9K), and average review rating (3.75).

Customer Segmentation:** Classifies the customer base into Loyal customers (3,116), Returning customers (701), and New customers (83).


Strategic Business Recommendations

Boost Subscriptions:** Promote exclusive member benefits to convert non-subscribers, who currently make up 73% of the customer base.

Customer Loyalty Programmes:** Implement targeted rewards to transition repeat buyers into the stable 'Loyal' segment.

Optimise Discount Policies:** Carefully balance promotional frequencies on high-elasticity items (such as hats and sneakers) to safeguard profit margins.
Targeted Marketing:** Focus acquisition campaigns and express shipping promotions on high-revenue demographics like Young Adults.
